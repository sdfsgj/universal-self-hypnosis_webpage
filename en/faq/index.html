FAQ

Common questions about 8 Hz.

What is 8 Hz?
8 Hz is a general wellness app built around quiet audio experiences — designed to support rest, relaxation, sleep preparation and everyday wellbeing.
What is 8 Hz designed for?
For the quieter moments: winding down in the evening, preparing for sleep, taking a pause during the day. It is designed to support relaxation, not to demand attention.
Is 8 Hz medical treatment?
No. 8 Hz is a general wellness product. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical or psychological condition. If you have persistent concerns about sleep, anxiety or mood, please consult a qualified professional.
Do I need headphones?
Headphones are recommended for the full experience, but not required. Any quiet way of listening works.
How loud should I listen?
Keep the volume low — just audible and comfortable. 8 Hz is designed to be quiet; there is no benefit to turning it up.
What is Somewhere?
Somewhere is a part of the 8 Hz app where sound belongs to a place — a quiet map of moments and atmospheres you can discover inside the app.
How does Co-create work?
Co-create is a simple community board where anyone can browse ideas. Signing in with your email lets you share an idea, support ideas you like, and comment. One level of comments, no noise.
Where can I download 8 Hz?
8 Hz is coming soon. When it launches, App Store and Google Play links will appear here and on the homepage.
