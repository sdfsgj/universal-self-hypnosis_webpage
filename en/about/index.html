Why does 8 Hz exist?

Because attention has become the most contested resource of ordinary life. Every screen asks for a little more of it, and quiet has become something you have to go looking for.

8 Hz exists to give some of that quiet back. It is a small, carefully made space — built around sound — where rest, relaxation and sleep preparation are treated as worth designing for.

We are not trying to hold your attention. We are trying to give it somewhere softer to land.
