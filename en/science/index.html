Designed with care.

8 Hz is a general wellness product. This page explains how we think about audio, rest, sleep preparation and self-hypnosis — carefully, and without medical claims.

Our Approach

8 Hz is designed to support relaxation and everyday wellbeing. Our work is evidence-informed: we read the published research on sound, rest and attention, and we let it guide design decisions. We do not claim clinical outcomes.

Audio & Relaxation

Slow, spacious sound can help some people unwind. Research on music, ambient sound and paced audio suggests links with relaxation and perceived calm. Individual experiences vary, and we design for that variance — gentle, optional, never demanding.

Sleep Preparation

8 Hz is designed to support the transition toward sleep: quieter sound, dimmer visuals, slower pacing. It is intended to support an evening wind-down, not to treat insomnia or any sleep disorder.

Self-hypnosis

Self-hypnosis, as a concept, refers to guided, self-directed states of focused relaxation. Some of our audio draws on this tradition of guided relaxation language. We present it as a relaxation practice — nothing more.

8 Hz

Our name refers to a frequency near the slow edge of human brainwave ranges often discussed in relaxation research — a quiet threshold between wakefulness and rest. It is a namesake and an intention, not a promise of brainwave change.

Wellness Check-ins

Simple, optional reflections inside the app help you notice how you feel before and after listening. They are wellness reflections, not assessments or diagnoses.

Content Review

Every script and soundscape is reviewed for tone, pacing and language before release. We avoid claims we cannot support, and we revise content when the evidence or our standards change.

Safety

8 Hz is not a medical product and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. If you are experiencing persistent difficulties with sleep, anxiety or mood, please speak with a qualified professional.

Research & Sources

We will publish the sources that inform our approach here as they are reviewed and confirmed. Nothing will be cited that we have not read.
