Safety

8 Hz is a general wellness product. Please read this page to understand what that means.

What 8 Hz is

8 Hz offers audio experiences designed to support relaxation, sleep preparation and everyday wellbeing. It is intended for general wellness use.

What 8 Hz is not

8 Hz is not a medical device, not medical treatment, and not a substitute for professional care. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, including insomnia, anxiety or depression.

Listening safely

Listen at a low, comfortable volume. Do not use 8 Hz while driving or in any situation that requires your full attention. If any content makes you uncomfortable, stop listening.

When to seek help

If you are struggling with persistent sleep problems, anxiety, low mood or thoughts of self-harm, please reach out to a qualified professional or local support services. 8 Hz is not an emergency resource.
