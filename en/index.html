What is 8 Hz

A quieter space, when you need one.

8 Hz is a wellness app built around sound. Quiet audio experiences, thoughtful design and calm interaction — created to support relaxation, sleep preparation and moments of calm in ordinary days.

Nothing here asks for your attention. You open it, listen, and let the interface slowly step back.

Why 8 Hz

Made for minds that are already full.

The interface can slowly step out of the way.

Modern life is a constant stream of notifications, information, content and screens — everything asking for a little more attention.

8 Hz is designed to be the opposite: an app that does not compete for you, but makes room for you.
